# Ajaia Sprint Planning & Team Goals

Welcome to the internal sprint planning document.

## High-Priority Deliverables
- **Document Creation:** Seamless rich-text editing with headings, lists, bold, and italic styles.
- **File Upload & Ingestion:** Convert `.docx`, `.md`, and `.txt` files directly into editable documents.
- **Granular Sharing:** Clear separation between **Owner**, **Editor**, and **Viewer** access levels.
- **Reliable Persistence:** Fast local storage with sub-second auto-save.

> "Shipping quality productivity software requires relentless focus on developer experience and user ergonomics."

### Key Metrics
1. Sub-100ms response times for document retrieval.
2. 100% test coverage across permission boundaries.
3. Zero-friction persona switching for product reviews.
